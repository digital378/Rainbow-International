World Cup 2026 Primer

Forty-eight teams, three host nations, one hundred and four matches. This is the largest World Cup in history and a rich, ready-made classroom resource for students of every age.

Tip: Use group-stage standings as a live maths worksheet — wins (3 pts), draws (1 pt), losses (0 pts).

This is also the first time three countries have co-hosted the World Cup. The USA, Mexico, and Canada spread matches across 16 cities and multiple time zones, making it a natural geography exercise too.

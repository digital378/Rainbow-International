# Marketing Dashboard — Quiet Blue Visual Refresh

## Goal

Make the `/marketing-27-28` overview feel neater and more soothing while retaining the established `/alliances` dashboard character.

## Approved Direction

Quiet Blue: a cool mist page background, crisp white surfaces, desaturated navy and steel-blue chart colours, pale slate supporting tones, and one restrained warm accent.

## Scope

Restyle:

- Dashboard page background
- KPI cards
- Monthly lead-volume chart card
- Status-breakdown chart card

Do not change:

- Dashboard navigation or tabs
- Filters
- Passcode flow
- Data fetching, aggregation, or refresh behavior
- Other dashboard views or reporting logic

## KPI Cards

- Use white cards with thin cool-grey borders and restrained shadows.
- Increase breathing room and keep labels quiet but legible.
- Use desaturated metric accents rather than vivid red, purple, green, or amber.
- Preserve all existing values, labels, and supporting text.

## Monthly Lead Volume

- Keep the existing monthly data and responsive chart behavior.
- Use a desaturated steel/navy treatment.
- Reduce grid-line contrast and soften axis labels.
- Keep values easy to compare without visually dominating the page.

## Status Breakdown

- Replace external pie labels with a compact donut chart.
- Put a tidy legend inside the card, with status name and count aligned in rows.
- Use accessible, muted colours that remain distinguishable.
- Preserve the existing tooltip and underlying values.

## Responsive Behavior

- Keep the two chart cards side by side on desktop.
- Stack chart cards cleanly on smaller screens.
- Prevent legends or labels from overflowing their cards.
- Maintain readable touch and text sizing.

## Acceptance Checks

- The overview clearly resembles the approved Quiet Blue preview.
- All existing KPI and chart values remain unchanged.
- No pie labels collide with card edges.
- Desktop and mobile layouts remain usable.
- Type checking and existing dashboard tests pass.

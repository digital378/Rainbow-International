Also check these before saving the full action:

Every operation has a unique operationId.
Every response has application/json.
All required parameters have clear types and enums where applicable.
No endpoint response schema is overly complex or inconsistent.
Avoid OpenAPI features ChatGPT may not handle well, such as very deep nested schemas, oneOf, anyOf, circular references, or nullable ambiguity.
Ensure the OpenAPI file is served with a clean YAML response, not HTML or redirect content.
Test the schema in the GPT editor after every batch, not only at the end.

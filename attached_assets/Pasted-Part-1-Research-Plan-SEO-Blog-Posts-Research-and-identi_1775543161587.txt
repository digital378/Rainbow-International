Part 1: Research & Plan SEO Blog Posts

Research and identify 8 high-search-volume blog post topics relevant to Rainbow International School (RIS) — a CBSE school in Thane, Maharashtra, India. The topics should target keywords that parents actively search when looking for schools, making education decisions, or seeking parenting/academic guidance.

Focus on topics with:

High search volume and clear parent intent (informational + commercial)
Relevance to school admissions, choosing schools, academic guidance, and parenting
Local SEO potential (Thane-specific where appropriate)
Opportunity to naturally position RIS as the answer
Think about what parents Google when:

Choosing between schools or boards (CBSE/ICSE/State)
Preparing for school admissions
Helping their child academically or emotionally
Understanding education policies or trends
Looking for school-related advice in Thane
For each blog post, define:

Title (SEO-optimized, compelling for parents)
Slug
Primary + secondary target keywords
Content outline (what the article covers)
Estimated word count (1,800-2,500 words each)
Content format (numbered lists, comparison tables, checklists, timelines, FAQ sections, etc.)
Part 2: Create the Blog Posts

After finalizing the 8 topics, create all blog posts following the same pattern used in the existing blog system. Each post should:

Be stored in the blog data structure (same pattern as existing blog posts in blog-post.tsx)
Have 1,800-2,500 words of genuinely useful, detailed content — not filler
Use proper markdown formatting: ## for h2 headings, ### for h3, **bold** for emphasis, bullet lists, numbered lists
Include a FAQ section at the end using **Q: / A: format for FAQ schema
End with a RIS_BACKLINK: entry linking to Rainbow Preschool (sister school) — use the format RIS_BACKLINK:Looking for early education? Visit [Rainbow Preschool International](https://www.rainbowpreschools.com) for Playgroup, Nursery & Kindergarten in Thane.
Include natural internal links to other RIS pages (admissions, programmes, contact) within the content
Cross-link between the new blog posts where relevant
Part 3: Register Everything

Register all 8 blog posts in the blog listing page
Add bot SSR entries with BlogPosting schema for each post
Add all blog URLs to sitemap.xml
Update llms.txt with the new blog section
Ensure the blog listing page displays all new posts with proper titles, descriptions, and thumbnails

Redesign and optimize the Rainbow International School home page to improve admission enquiries, campus visit bookings, WhatsApp conversations, and parent trust.

Page URL:
https://rainbowinternationalschool.in/

Primary objective:
Improve the current home page without changing the core brand identity. The page should look premium, load faster, and clearly guide parents toward enquiry, WhatsApp, campus visit booking, and admission application.

Target audience:
Parents in Thane and nearby areas searching for a reputed CBSE school from Nursery to Class 12.

Core positioning:
Rainbow International School is a CBSE-affiliated Nursery to Class 12 school in Thane, established in 2009, with a 3.5-acre campus, strong academics, sports, safety, co-curricular exposure, transport support, and a parent-friendly school environment.

The current page already has a premium blue theme, student background image, admissions open top bar, phone number, location, enquiry button, quick admission enquiry form, WhatsApp CTA, and trust cards such as Since 2009, 3.5 Acres, CBSE Affiliation, and student count.
